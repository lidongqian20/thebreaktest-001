===== SAMPLE: The Productivity Lie (Fj-INVmmILo, unknown, hank-recent-01) =====
Last Chance at Pizzamas Dot Com: https://pizzamas.com/

The Everything We Have Too Much Of Bundle: https://pizzamas.com/products/everything-we-have-too-much-of-bundle

Productivity when applied to individual creativity, I think, is exactly backward. I think we all should want to make stuff...but the hard part isn't doing it as efficiently as possible...you can do whatever you want once you're making stuff...the hard part is figuring out how to get yourself making. Focusing on "productivity" in that situation is crazy talk...instead, you need to figure out what opportunities you have and what matters to you.

===== SAMPLE: Why Are We So Bad at Executing People? (AvNZfUjYYOk, unknown, hank-recent-05) =====
We are very bad at executing people. Depending on how you count, something like 7% of lethal injections in the US have been botched. This seemed very strange to me, so I dove into the numbers, looked at veterinary euthanasia, end-of-life care, and execution procedures, and what I found was that, almost certainly, the difference here is that a very high percentage of competent individuals refuse to engage in government executions.

I understand that, to many, killing a killer is a way of honoring life. I think that this has always been an ugly lie, but the fact that we cannot do it at all competently indicates to me that, as a society, we no longer actually believe it.
